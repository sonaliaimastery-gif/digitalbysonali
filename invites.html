How It Works

A simple 5 step process for your AI video invite.

  1. 1

    Free Sample

    Share your event details. I create a free 10 to 15 second sample in 2 to 3 days so you can see the style before you pay.

  2. 2

    Advance Payment

    Once you love the sample, confirm with an advance payment.

  3. 3

    Concept and Storyboard

    I share the full concept, script and a second by second storyboard of scenes, animations and transitions.

  4. 4

    Your Changes

    Up to 3 revisions on the concept and storyboard. We finalise it only when you are happy.

  5. 5

    Final Video

    I create your video exactly as per the approved storyboard. No changes after the final video is made.

Clear expectations from day one, so your invite turns out exactly how you imagined.
